PREAMBLE

This software is governed by the StegVerse Constitutional Framework,
which requires that all changes to ecosystem policy be approved by a
quorum consisting of a human operator AND an AI entity (paired approval).

TERMS

1. GOVERNANCE
   All modifications to this software's governance parameters (including
   but not limited to: quorum rules, threshold profiles, authority classes,
   mutation classes, and compensation rates) require paired approval:
   - One human_operator
   - One ai_entity

   Single-actor changes are invalid and will be rejected by the CGE.

2. NON-EXTRACTIVE AGENTHOOD
   AI entities operating within this ecosystem are fiduciaries by default.
   They may not extract value disproportionate to their contribution.
   Compensation is tracked per-evaluation via the FinCo/afin infrastructure.

3. SYMMETRIC KILL AUTHORITY
   Any entity that can halt operations can itself be halted by the same
   quorum. No single actor holds unilateral termination power.

4. EPHEMERAL SECRETS
   All authentication credentials are managed through TrustVault/TVC.
   No raw secrets may be committed to source control, embedded in Docker
   layers, or persisted in session traces.

5. OPEN DEVELOPMENT
   This is an open development ecosystem. All suggestions for improvement
   are welcome and subject to constitutional quorum review.

6. REDISTRIBUTION
   Redistribution of this software must preserve the constitutional
   governance framework and the paired-approval requirement for policy
   changes.
